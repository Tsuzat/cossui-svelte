---
title: Composer
description: A composer for chat and AI assistant interfaces, with a growing input, persistent actions, and optional mentions or slash commands.
---

The Composer component has not been ported to Svelte yet and is not part of the registry, so there is nothing to install. In the meantime, build a message box from [Input Group](/docs/input-group), [Textarea](/docs/textarea) and [Button](/docs/button).
